## Original formal business description

### LIFE TRANSITIONS SUPPORT PARTNERS

A Nurse-Led Initiative | Nairobi, Kenya

### Business Overview

Life Transitions Support Partners is a nurse-led community health and psychosocial support organization committed to ensuring that no individual or family navigates major life transitions alone.

We provide holistic, home-based, and family-centered support that extends beyond clinical care, addressing the psychological, educational, spiritual, and practical needs of individuals and families during critical life phases including childbirth, caregiving, chronic and terminal illness, bereavement, and post-hospital recovery.

### Our Core Services

#### Psychological Support

We offer professional psychosocial preparation and support for childbirth and motherhood, counseling for individuals and families facing terminal illness, and specialized guidance on breaking difficult and sensitive news to vulnerable populations, including children.

#### Health Education and Caregiver Training

We provide structured, competency-based training for:

- Domestic Managers on child care and basic resuscitation
- Nannies on safe and comprehensive baby care
- Home Caregivers on evidence-based care of the bedridden at home

#### Spiritual Care Services - Offered Free of Charge

we provide complimentary spiritual care services including home visitation following bereavement, support in communicating loss to children, and family therapy and counseling.

#### Support Groups Linkage

We facilitate linkage of clients to appropriate peer support groups comprising individuals with similar lived experiences to foster shared learning, emotional support, and community resilience.

#### Professional Health Linkage

We provide referral and linkage to a vetted network of qualified health professionals for specialized clinical and therapeutic interventions.

#### Home Follow-up and Continuity of Care

We conduct structured home follow-up visits post-hospital discharge for clients with long-term illness to assess coping mechanisms, evaluate caregiver compliance, reinforce health education, and provide guidance on financial goal adjustment following provider illness.

### Our Commitment

Life Transitions Support Partners is founded on the principles of compassion, professionalism, confidentiality, and trustworthiness. We are dedicated to improving health outcomes, reducing caregiver burden, preventing avoidable hospital readmissions, and restoring dignity and hope to families during vulnerable transitions.

Tagline: No one should navigate life's transitions alone.

## Content boundaries

- The source establishes a nurse-led initiative; it does not establish a registered legal form, charity status, hospital licence, accreditation, or verified staff credentials.
- The commitments about improved outcomes and reduced readmissions express the initiative's aims. Publish measured results only when supporting evidence exists.
- The founder's free spiritual-care description must survive editing. Resolve any overlap with paid counselling explicitly rather than silently charging for activities described as complimentary.
- Clarify whether "provider illness" means illness of the household breadwinner or something else. Do not rewrite it as a settled interpretation.
- Psychological support, spiritual care, family therapy, financial guidance, training, and specialist clinical care need understandable boundaries and accurate descriptions of the responsible professionals.
- Website copy may use gentler language, such as helping caregivers put a care plan into practice. Such copy remains a proposal until reviewed.
